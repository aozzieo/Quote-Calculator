Why does adding an employee lower my project quote?When you hire a full-time employee, your total billable capacity increases. More hours to spread overhead across means each hour costs less — so the per-project price drops. The tradeoff: you now need more projects per year to cover their salary. The Dashboard shows whether the numbers work out at your current project volume.
True cost multiplier guide1.25× — no benefits, minimal state tax. 1.30× — standard benefits (recommended default). 1.35–1.40× — rich benefits, high workers' comp, paid time off.
